Certified Structural Quality

OUR FABRICATION STANDARDS

Every gate, shed, roof, and stair is fabricated with genuine certified steel components to ensure decades of structural integrity without sagging or corrosion.

01

IS 2062 Grade Prime Steel

We source only certified structural hollow sections (SHS, RHS, CHS) and heavy channels from primary steel manufacturers (Tata Structura, Apollo, Jindal). Zero sub-standard scrap tubes.

02

AWS D1.1 Full-Penetration MIG Welds

Fabricated with shielded gas Metal Inert Gas (MIG) welding systems. Penetration is verified for maximum mechanical shear resistance without weak cold-lap joints or porous seams.

03

Anti-Rust Zinc Phosphate Primer

Every welded seam is ground flush, acid-washed, and coated with a high-solids zinc phosphate anti-rust primer before applying dual topcoats of weatherproof polyurethane enamel.
